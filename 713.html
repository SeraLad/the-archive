SCP-713

THE DISCONNECTER

SECURE • CONTAIN • PROTECT

Item #: SCP-713

Object Class: Euclid

Filed by: Dr. Sera

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-713 is housed in a standard humanoid containment suite equipped with approved personal effects and recreational accommodations. Due to SCP-713's cooperative behavior and lack of intentional hostility, no additional security measures are required under normal circumstances.

Personnel assigned to SCP-713 are instructed not to interpret periods of silence, withdrawal, or reduced communication as acts of aggression. SCP-713 has demonstrated a preference for temporary isolation during periods of emotional distress, and such requests should be respected whenever operationally possible.

If SCP-713 displays signs of significant emotional instability, including visible distress, agitation, or heightened anxiety, all interviews, psychological evaluations, and testing procedures are to be suspended immediately.

Personnel are prohibited from intentionally provoking emotional responses from SCP-713. Attempts to restrain, pursue, or continue interaction with SCP-713 during an anomalous event require approval from the acting Site Director.

Description

SCP-713, informally designated "The Disconnecter", is a humanoid entity physically resembling a human estimated to be between 17 and 19 years of age.

Medical examinations have failed to identify significant biological abnormalities. Genetic testing has produced inconclusive results regarding SCP-713's origin.

The circumstances surrounding SCP-713's initial discovery remain unknown. Foundation records regarding the entity's recovery are unavailable or incomplete.

SCP-713 has confirmed that it was aware of its anomalous nature prior to Foundation contact but has provided no further information regarding the origin of this knowledge.

Behavioral Profile

SCP-713 demonstrates a cautious approach toward unfamiliar individuals. During initial interactions, the entity frequently avoids direct eye contact, reporting that prolonged eye contact increases feelings of nervousness and discomfort.

SCP-713 may appear defensive when upset or frustrated. However, observation indicates this behavior is a response to emotional stress rather than an attempt to intimidate or harm others.

Trust development with SCP-713 requires extended periods of consistent interaction. Attempts to accelerate this process have repeatedly resulted in increased withdrawal.

Once SCP-713 establishes trust with an individual, significant behavioral changes occur. The entity becomes more expressive, humorous, and conversational.

Foundation psychologists have determined that SCP-713 values sincerity, consistency, and time when forming interpersonal connections.

Interests and Social Behavior

When comfortable around trusted individuals, SCP-713 frequently shares unusual facts, personal observations, and information regarding various topics of interest.

SCP-713 demonstrates a strong interest in recreational media, particularly video games and films. Following completion of a film, SCP-713 commonly provides detailed commentary regarding themes, characters, story structure, and personal interpretations.

Researchers have noted that these discussions appear to be one of SCP-713's preferred methods of social interaction.

Relationship With The Foundation

SCP-713 displays an overall indifferent attitude toward the Foundation as an organization. The entity has not expressed significant fear, hatred, or attachment toward Foundation authority.

However, SCP-713 has demonstrated curiosity regarding Foundation procedures, specifically regarding the reasoning behind experiments and evaluations conducted on the entity.

Dr. Sera: "You don't seem bothered by the fact that you're being studied."

SCP-713: "Not really. I'm more curious why you're studying me."

Anomalous Properties

SCP-713's anomalous effects manifest exclusively during periods of heightened emotional distress.

When experiencing intense negative emotions, SCP-713 instinctively attempts to distance itself from nearby individuals and seek isolation.

If SCP-713 is prevented from isolating itself, a localized neurological phenomenon occurs within a radius of approximately 1 to 20 meters, dependent on the severity of SCP-713's emotional state.

  • Difficulty concentrating
  • Reduced short-term memory retention
  • Slowed cognitive processing
  • Headaches
  • Dizziness
  • Emotional detachment
  • Spontaneous nosebleeds
  • Temporary loss of consciousness

No permanent neurological damage has been documented.

Current research suggests SCP-713's emotional state causes measurable changes in surrounding brain activity. The exact mechanism remains unknown.

Personal Acceptance

Unlike many anomalous entities, SCP-713 does not appear to view its abilities as a curse or unwanted condition.

SCP-713 acknowledges its anomalous nature and accepts it as part of itself. However, the entity has expressed concern regarding the possibility of unintentionally harming others during emotional episodes.

Psychological Restrictions

[REDACTED BY ORDER OF SITE DIRECTOR]

Information regarding SCP-713's past experiences, the origin of its defensive behaviors, and factors contributing to its emotional responses is restricted to Level 4 personnel.

Researcher's Note — Dr. Sera

"SCP-713 is frequently misunderstood during initial observation. Personnel often mistake silence, avoidance, or defensiveness for hostility."

"SCP-713 is not dangerous because it wishes to cause harm. It is dangerous because it is an individual whose emotional state can unintentionally affect those around it."

"The distinction is important."

"Understanding SCP-713 requires patience."

— Dr. Sera
