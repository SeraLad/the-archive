DOCUMENT ACCESS: SCP-713

SCP-713 — "THE DISCONNECTER"

DOCUMENT STATUS: ACTIVE

OBJECT CLASS: EUCLID

CLEARANCE REQUIRED: LEVEL 4

SPECIAL CONTAINMENT PROCEDURES

SCP-713 is currently contained within a standard humanoid containment chamber at a Foundation-controlled facility. Personnel assigned to SCP-713 must maintain calm and controlled communication during all interactions. Attempts to intentionally provoke emotional responses from SCP-713 are strictly prohibited. If SCP-713 displays signs of emotional instability, testing procedures are to be immediately suspended until approval is granted by senior Foundation staff. Personnel are reminded that SCP-713's abilities appear to be involuntary and connected to emotional distress.

DESCRIPTION

SCP-713 is a humanoid entity resembling a human individual estimated to be between 17-19 years of age. The entity possesses black hair and blue eyes, with no confirmed physical abnormalities separating SCP-713 from baseline humans. SCP-713 displays behaviors commonly associated with anxiety, including avoiding eye contact, difficulty trusting unfamiliar individuals, and defensive behavior when distressed. Despite these traits, SCP-713 has demonstrated the ability to form strong connections with individuals it considers trustworthy.

ANOMALOUS PROPERTIES

SCP-713's anomalous effects manifest primarily during periods of extreme emotional instability. Individuals within an estimated range of 1-20 meters may experience:

  • Temporary loss of consciousness
  • Nosebleeding
  • Difficulty maintaining focus
  • Temporary emotional detachment
  • Short-term memory disruption

The severity of these effects appears directly connected to SCP-713's emotional state.

DISCOVERY LOG 713

DATE: ██/██/2014

LOCATION: █████████████

Foundation agents were dispatched following reports of multiple civilians experiencing sudden unconsciousness during an unidentified emotional incident. Upon arrival, agents discovered SCP-713 displaying visible distress while attempting to assist affected individuals. Initial investigation resulted in SCP-713 being classified as an anomalous humanoid entity.

INTERVIEW LOG 713-A

INTERVIEWER: Dr. Sera

SUBJECT: SCP-713

Dr. Sera: "Do you understand why the Foundation is keeping you here?"

SCP-713: "...Because people get hurt when I lose control."

Dr. Sera: "Do you want to hurt people?"

SCP-713: "No."

END LOG

INCIDENT REPORT 713-02

During an unauthorized attempt by personnel to trigger SCP-713's anomalous abilities, the entity experienced severe emotional distress. Multiple staff members reported temporary unconsciousness, headaches, and memory inconsistencies. Following this incident, all experiments involving intentional emotional provocation were permanently suspended.
