SPECIAL CONTAINMENT PROCEDURES

SCP-713 is currently contained within a standard humanoid containment chamber at a Foundation-controlled facility. Personnel assigned to SCP-713 are required to maintain calm and controlled communication during all interactions. Attempts to intentionally provoke SCP-713's emotional state are strictly prohibited. If SCP-713 begins displaying signs of emotional instability, all testing procedures are to be suspended until approval is granted by senior Foundation staff. Personnel are reminded that SCP-713's anomalous effects appear to be unintentional and directly connected to emotional distress.

DESCRIPTION

SCP-713 is a humanoid entity resembling a human individual estimated to be between 17-19 years of age. The entity possesses black hair and blue eyes, with no confirmed biological abnormalities separating SCP-713 from baseline humans. SCP-713 has demonstrated behaviors commonly associated with anxiety, including avoiding eye contact, hesitation during conversation, and defensive reactions when distressed. The entity requires extended periods of time to develop trust with unfamiliar individuals. Despite initial difficulties communicating with Foundation personnel, SCP-713 has demonstrated the ability to form strong emotional connections with trusted individuals.

BEHAVIORAL NOTES

SCP-713 has shown a preference for calm environments and familiar individuals. The entity has expressed interest in recreational activities including video games and movies. Foundation researchers have noted that SCP-713 does not display hostile intent toward personnel and appears distressed by the possibility of harming others. Further study into SCP-713's emotional response patterns is ongoing.

ANOMALOUS PROPERTIES

SCP-713's anomalous effects manifest primarily during periods of extreme emotional instability. When SCP-713 experiences severe distress, individuals within an estimated range of 1-20 meters may experience:

  • Temporary loss of consciousness
  • Nosebleeding
  • Difficulty maintaining focus
  • Temporary emotional detachment
  • Short-term memory disruption

The severity of these effects appears directly related to SCP-713's emotional state.

DISCOVERY LOG 713

DATE: ██/██/2014

LOCATION: █████████████

Foundation personnel were dispatched following reports of multiple civilians experiencing sudden unconsciousness during an unidentified emotional incident. Upon arrival, agents discovered SCP-713 displaying visible distress while attempting to assist affected individuals. Initial investigation resulted in SCP-713 being classified as an anomalous humanoid entity.

INTERVIEW LOG 713-A

INTERVIEWER: Dr. Sera

SUBJECT: SCP-713

Dr. Sera: "Do you understand why the Foundation is keeping you here?"

SCP-713: "...Because people get hurt when I lose control."

Dr. Sera: "Do you want to hurt people?"

SCP-713: "No."

END LOG

INCIDENT REPORT 713-02

During an unauthorized attempt by personnel to trigger SCP-713's anomalous abilities, the entity experienced severe emotional distress. Multiple staff members reported temporary unconsciousness, headaches, and memory inconsistencies. Following this incident, all experiments involving intentional emotional provocation were permanently suspended.
