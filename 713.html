SPECIAL CONTAINMENT PROCEDURES

SCP-713 is currently contained within a humanoid containment chamber designed to provide a stable and low-stress environment. Personnel interacting with SCP-713 are required to maintain calm and respectful communication. Attempts to intentionally cause emotional distress or provoke SCP-713's anomalous abilities are strictly prohibited. Foundation research has shown that trust and familiarity significantly reduce the likelihood of anomalous events. Personnel assigned to SCP-713 are encouraged to establish communication and maintain a consistent routine.

DESCRIPTION

SCP-713 is a humanoid entity resembling a baseline human individual, estimated to be between 17-19 years of age. The entity possesses black hair and blue eyes with no confirmed physical abnormalities. SCP-713 displays behaviors commonly associated with nervousness and social anxiety, including avoiding eye contact, hesitation during conversation, and defensive reactions when overwhelmed. The entity requires extended periods of time to trust unfamiliar individuals. Despite initial difficulty forming connections, SCP-713 has demonstrated a strong ability to care for individuals it considers close.

BEHAVIORAL PROFILE

SCP-713 is considered non-hostile. The entity has shown interest in ordinary human activities, including video games and movies. SCP-713 appears to value emotional connection and understanding above personal safety. Researchers have observed that the entity's greatest concern is not causing harm to others.

ANOMALOUS PROPERTIES

SCP-713's anomalous abilities appear connected to emotional instability. During periods of extreme distress, individuals within an estimated range of 1-20 meters may experience:

  • Temporary loss of consciousness
  • Nosebleeding
  • Difficulty concentrating
  • Temporary emotional detachment
  • Short-term memory disruption

The effects are believed to be involuntary and are not considered intentional attacks.

DISCOVERY LOG 713

DATE: ██/██/2014

LOCATION: █████████████

Foundation personnel were alerted after reports of multiple individuals experiencing sudden physical and emotional disturbances during an unknown event. Upon investigation, SCP-713 was discovered attempting to assist those affected despite experiencing distress itself. Following observation and testing, SCP-713 was classified as an anomalous humanoid entity.

RESEARCHER NOTES

Researcher: Dr. Sera

SCP-713 is an unusual case within Foundation records. The entity does not display the behavior of an aggressive anomaly. Instead, SCP-713 appears to be an individual who fears causing harm more than anything else.

Through continued interaction, SCP-713 and assigned researcher Dr. Sera have developed a mutual level of trust. This relationship has shown that understanding and patience are often more effective than traditional containment methods.

SCP-713 is not defined by its abilities. It is defined by the connections it chooses to protect.

INTERVIEW LOG 713-A

INTERVIEWER: Dr. Sera

SUBJECT: SCP-713

Dr. Sera: "Do you understand why the Foundation is keeping you here?"

SCP-713: "...Because people get hurt when I lose control."

Dr. Sera: "Do you want to hurt people?"

SCP-713: "No."

FINAL RESEARCH NOTE

Dr. Sera: "SCP-713 is not simply an anomaly to be contained. It is someone who needed a place where they could be understood."
