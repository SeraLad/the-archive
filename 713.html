SPECIAL CONTAINMENT PROCEDURES

SCP-713 is to be housed within a humanoid containment chamber designed to maintain a calm and stable environment. Personnel assigned to SCP-713 are required to avoid unnecessary emotional stressors and maintain respectful communication. Attempts to intentionally trigger SCP-713's anomalous abilities are strictly prohibited. Research has shown that trust, familiarity, and emotional stability greatly reduce the likelihood of anomalous events.

DESCRIPTION

SCP-713 is a humanoid entity estimated to be between 17-19 years of age. The entity possesses black hair and blue eyes with no known physical abnormalities. SCP-713 displays behaviors including nervousness, avoidance of eye contact, hesitation during unfamiliar interactions, and defensive responses when overwhelmed. Despite initial difficulty trusting others, SCP-713 has demonstrated strong loyalty and care toward individuals it considers close.

ANOMALOUS PROPERTIES

SCP-713's anomalous effects occur during periods of extreme emotional distress. Individuals within an estimated range of 1-20 meters may experience:

  • Temporary unconsciousness
  • Nosebleeding
  • Difficulty focusing
  • Emotional detachment
  • Temporary memory disruption

The Foundation currently believes these effects are involuntary. SCP-713 has shown consistent concern regarding the possibility of harming others.

BEHAVIORAL NOTES

SCP-713 demonstrates interest in ordinary human activities, including video games and movies. The entity responds positively to individuals who demonstrate patience and understanding. Long-term observation suggests that SCP-713 does not desire isolation, but fears causing harm to those around it.

DISCOVERY LOG

Following reports of unexplained emotional disturbances affecting multiple civilians, Foundation personnel discovered SCP-713 during an event involving an uncontrolled anomalous manifestation. The entity was found attempting to assist affected individuals rather than causing harm. Further investigation resulted in SCP-713 being classified as a humanoid anomaly.
