RESEARCHER NOTE

Researcher: Dr. Sera

SCP-713 remains one of the most unusual cases within my research. When first observed, the Foundation saw an anomaly capable of disconnecting individuals from their surroundings. However, continued interaction revealed something different.

SCP-713 is not an entity seeking to harm others. It is an entity afraid of losing connection with them.
